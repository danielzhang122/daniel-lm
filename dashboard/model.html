What's going on here?

Imagine the AI is playing guess the next word: ?The cat sat on the??

The dots are like tiny workers passing numbers along. The lines are their connections. Each line has a number called a weight; training adjusts these numbers when the model makes a bad guess.

  • Dots are input features, hidden neurons, and output features.
  • Lines show connections. Brighter means a bigger weight magnitude.
  • Red and blue mean positive and negative weights, not good and bad.
  • Hover or click a dot to follow its connections. Choose a block to see a different part of the model.

This is a small sample of the model, not live neuron activity. Press Explore to load weights from the latest saved checkpoint.
